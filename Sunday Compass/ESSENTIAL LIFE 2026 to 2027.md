# ESSENTIAL LIFE: 2026 to 2027

## Goals
- To be a YouTuber and teacher who earns a living from his content
- To be a coach people happily pay because the work changes their lives
- To be someone who finishes what he starts
- To be a present partner who grows with her creatively, mentally and emotionally
- To be a dad who is there for the small stuff, like the school run
- To be strong, fit and healthy for a long life
- To be a reader who never stops learning
- To be a jack of all trades who builds and fixes things with his hands
- To be financially free
- To be a calm, clear-minded person who meditates daily
- To be a friend who makes time for long lunches and real conversations
- To be the owner of a home that looks after itself

## Purpose
I help people in their 20s and 30s find their path and make their first dollar from their passion, because I've lived that search myself and I love teaching what I learn.

## Policies
- I don't spend on impulse. Anything non-essential waits before I buy it, because my money goes toward the life I'm building, not the one I'm drifting through.
- I don't give my help away without naming a price first, because my time is worth paying for.

## Plans (next 12 months)

- I have my full business and product setup running: a coaching offer, products and a way to get paid.
- I am publishing a YouTube video every week.
- I am running marketing, retargeting and an active Instagram presence.

## Essential Scene
**Home and time.** I am living in a sustainable four-bedroom home, almost off-grid, with solar passive design, internet and emergency grid power, because I want a home that looks after itself and leaves me free for what matters. I am naturally awake at 6:30, outside in the sun, meditating for 20 minutes, because a clear mind sets the tone for the day. I am working under 25 hours a week, with Fridays off for my kids and me, because I choose what I spend time on and what I don't.

**Work and money.** I am creating content for a business I love, from anywhere, because teaching and telling stories gives me purpose. I am publishing one long-form piece a week, a newsletter article plus a talking-head YouTube video, and my channel reaches about 1 million views a year, because consistency builds an evergreen library that keeps bringing people in. I am earning $300k a year, because my income comes from the value I create, not the hours I put in:
- Coaching, about $96k. I am coaching eight ongoing clients at $999 a month, because paid coaching lets me go deep with people who are committed.
- Online products, $144k. I am selling courses and info products at around $200 each, about 720 sales a year, because they help far more people than I could ever coach one to one.
- Sponsorships and affiliates, $50k. I am partnering with software and products I already use and recommend, because my audience trusts my recommendations.
- YouTube AdSense, $10k. This is roughly what 1 million views pays, because views are the front door, not the main income.

I am owning two investment properties with my partner and over $100k in stocks, because I never want to have to work for money by 40.

**Body.** I am training three days a week for at least an hour and moving 30 to 60 minutes every day, because it protects my strength, my heart and my mind. I am eating mostly vegetables with a rotation of proteins, and sleeping 7 to 8 hours without an alarm, because sleep is the heartbeat of everything else.

**People.** I am spending time with my partner every day, plus a date night and a date day every week, because we grow together. I am taking 90 minutes or more each day for a long lunch with someone I love, because challenging, stimulating relationships make me better. I am riding my kids to school on the ebike, because I want to be there for the small stuff.

**Learning.** I am reading for at least an hour a day and tinkering with woodworking, construction, mechanical repairs, permaculture and land care, because understanding how things work is my passion and I love working with my hands.

## Anti-Essential Scene
I am 38 and working a job I hate to service a mortgage, tired every day, with little time for my kids and no money to spare. Ascentivity faded years ago, and I told myself I was too busy with the kids. I still think about it all the time. When a friend asks whether I ever did anything with YouTube and teaching, I say "life got in the way", and I feel guilty, embarrassed and regretful, because I know I stopped trying and let small excuses stack up.
